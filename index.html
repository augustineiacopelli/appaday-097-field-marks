Specimen intake

Photograph what you found. Get the field marks that name it.

Point at a bird, mammal, insect, reptile, plant, or fungus. Field Marks returns the diagnostic features an identification actually rests on, the species it is most often confused with, and a plain warning when the thing in front of you can hurt you.

Take a photo, choose one from your library, or drop a file here.

Field journal

Field Marks is a study aid, not an authority. Every identification it returns is a best reading of one photograph and should be confirmed against a regional field guide, a local extension office, or a naturalist before you act on it.

It never tells you whether something is safe to eat, and it never will. Foraging decisions are made with a physical guide and an experienced person, not a phone.
